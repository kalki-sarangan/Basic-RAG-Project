## Question 1

**Question:**
What is PROACT?

**Generated Answer:**
PROACT is a framework for human-robot collaborative transport developed by the authors. It integrates predictions of human collaborative behavior with compliant robot control to address obstacles in collaborative transport. The core idea is to incorporate anticipatory behavior into compliant whole-body control, making the robot's decision-making process aware of the constraints imposed by humans.

## Question 2

**Question:**
What is TAILOR and what problem does it address?
    
**Generated Answer:**
TAILOR is a log parsing framework designed to improve template inference for rare log groups by enabling template preservation during training. It addresses the challenge of evaluating rare log groups by simulating word-RAMs using template-preserving augmentation techniques.
